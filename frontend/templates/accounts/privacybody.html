1. Scope & Commitment

At HER CARE+, we recognize that the physiological symptoms and mental wellness notes you log are deeply personal and sensitive. We are fundamentally committed to protecting your privacy and security. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how we manage it, and the rigorous safeguards we implement to defend your confidentiality.

We operate under an absolute security rule: Your physiological trackers and mental health records belong to YOU. They are never sold, rented, or commercialized.

2. Data We Collect

To deliver advanced health insights and period predictions, we collect categories of data directly input by you:

  • Account Credentials: Basic contact inputs, including first name, last name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Physiological Tracking Logs: Menstrual cycle dates, physiological symptoms (e.g. cramps, flow details), and energy metrics.
  • Mental Health Indicators: Mood notes, anxiety trackers, and daily journaling prompts entered on the Mental Health portal.
  • Wellness Logs: Nutritional entries, workout tracking times, and medication compliance schedules.

3. How We Use Data

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Generating localized cycle forecasting metrics and personalized symptoms trend charts.
  • Displaying historical mood summaries on your mental health timeline to support mindfulness patterns.
  • Dispatching automated notification alerts for scheduled appointments, prescriptions, and pill logs.
  • Optimizing application interfaces and performance metrics based on anonymized diagnostic feedback.

4. No Data Monetization

Unlike standard wellness tools, HER CARE+ is independent of data-monetization networks.

  • We do NOT sell or lease individual health metrics or personal details to third-party advertising companies.
  • We do NOT share physiological indicators with research institutes or insurance providers without your explicit, separate opt-in consent.
  • We only share relevant health summaries with external medical consultations that you explicitly schedule through our platform.

5. Security & Encryption

We deploy robust, state-of-the-art security safeguards designed to prevent data leaks or unauthorized interventions:

  • Data in Transit: All communications between your client device and HER CARE+ database clusters are strictly encrypted using high-grade TLS (Transport Layer Security) protocols.
  • Data at Rest: All user tracking logs and profile details are encrypted at rest using advanced AES-256 standards.
  • Mental Health Isolation: Mental health mood notes are isolated and encrypted with robust access control lists. They are accessible only via authenticated API sessions generated for your user account.

Our data systems are configured to align with secure clinical best practices, ensuring your physiological tracking assets are managed with the highest standard of virtual containment.

6. Your Data Rights

You have comprehensive authority over your personal tracking assets. Specifically, you have the right to:

  • Review and edit your data points at any time through the profile and logs interfaces.
  • Export your historical logging data in structured CSV or PDF formats (via our Health Reports dashboard).
  • Permanently purge your HER CARE+ user profile and all accompanying health history databases from our servers at any time.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may modify this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications to database storage conventions or legal requirements. When updates occur, we will adjust the "Last Updated" date at the header of this page. Continued utilization of HER CARE+ after modifications implies acceptance of adjusted terms.
