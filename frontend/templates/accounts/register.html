Privacy Policy

Your privacy, data security, and confidentiality are our highest priorities.

1. Information We Collect

To provide holistic health insights, HER CARE+ collects personal details (name, email, age) and physiological logs (cycle data, wellness reminders, and mental health mood history). We do not collect high-risk personal data without explicit request.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your health logs strictly to render customized analytics on your personal dashboard, predict cycle periods, and send notification reminders. We NEVER sell or share your individual health details with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and electronic security safeguards. All database assets are encrypted at rest, and HTTP exchanges run strictly over secure HTTPS protocols using TLS encryption.

4. Mental Health Mood Privacy

Mental health data has elevated privacy protection. Your mood history notes are isolated and encrypted with robust access control lists. They are accessible only via authenticated API sessions generated for your user account.
